Deval Kotak / Optiverse

A side quest that reached 150,000 students.

Before the security tooling and the market notebooks, there was this: a mentorship project co-founded with a friend and run entirely by students, for students. Dormant for now, with a relaunch being planned around everyone’s new commitments. This page is pulled from the team’s own Notion on every deploy.

Optiverse is a student-built mentorship project. No suits, no corporate off-site, just older students who've been through the noise helping younger ones figure out what they actually want.

We ran free 1-on-1 mentorship for students aged 11 to 17: self-reflection, honest conversation, zero pressure, a certificate at the end for the ones who like proof. Mentors were students too, scattered across 18+ countries, no salaries, just people who wanted to be useful.

Optiverse is dormant right now, and a relaunch is in planning around everyone's new commitments. If you want in when it comes back, join the waitlist and we'll reach out.

How it worked

Free. Always. Charging money filters out the kids who need it most, so we didn't. The whole program was 4 to 6 hours in total, online (offline if a mentor happened to be nearby), scheduled entirely around the student and mentor's own time. No script, no forced curriculum, just a mentor who'd let you talk without judging you for it.
