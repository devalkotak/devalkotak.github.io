A side quest that got 150,000 students

Before the security tooling and the market notebooks, there was this: a mentorship project co-founded with a friend and run entirely by students, for students. On pause, not dead. This page syncs from the source of truth, so it updates the moment we do.

Optiverse is a student-built mentorship project. No suits, no corporate off-site, just older students who've been through the noise helping younger ones figure out what they actually want.

We ran free 1-on-1 mentorship for students aged 10–18: self-reflection, honest conversation, zero pressure, a certificate at the end for the ones who like proof. Mentors were students too — scattered across 18+ countries, no salaries, just people who wanted to be useful.

Operations are paused indefinitely right now. Not dead — recharging. If you want in when it comes back, register and wait it out with us.

How it worked

Free. Always. Charging money filters out the kids who need it most, so we didn't. 4–6 hours, online (offline if a mentor happened to be nearby), scheduled entirely around the student and mentor's own time. No script, no forced curriculum — just a mentor who'd let you talk without judging you for it.
